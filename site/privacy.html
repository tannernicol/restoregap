Privacy

Restore Gap Cloud · effective 2026-10-08

The short version: the restoregap binary and this website collect nothing. Restore Gap Cloud stores what you push to it and the minimum needed to sign you in and bill you, and nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train anything.

The website and the binary

restoregap.com is a static page with no analytics, cookies, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. The restoregap binary makes no network requests except the drill commands you declare and restoregap bundle push, which only runs when you run it.

We do not read the contents of your bundles except as the service renders them to you, or to investigate a security incident or a support request you made.

Who else sees data

  • Stripe processes payments and sees your email and payment details. We never see card numbers.
  • Our email provider delivers sign-in links and alerts to the address you gave.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server and its encrypted disk. The current provider is named in the repository's docs/CLOUD.md.
  • People you share with. A share link shows the merged proof state and, for each bundle, its host name, context file names, evidence file paths and sizes. Give links only to people who may see that.

We do not sell data, do not share it for advertising, and disclose it to anyone else only when the law requires and we have told you where we can.

Your rights

You can export every bundle (each stored archive is the bytes you pushed), see everything in your workspace from the dashboard, correct your email by signing in with the new one, and delete your workspace by emailing us. Deletion removes all of the above within 30 days except what the law makes us keep, such as invoices. If you are in the EU, UK or a jurisdiction with similar rights, those rights apply as written in its law, and you may complain to your local authority.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Tokens are stored hashed. The service has no credentials to your machines and cannot request anything a bundle does not contain. Report a vulnerability through the repository's private reporting channel described in SECURITY.md.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we email you at least 30 days before the change applies.
