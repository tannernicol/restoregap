1. The service

Cloud receives signed bundles that you explicitly push with restoregap bundle push, stores them for your plan's retention period, shows the merged state of your enrolled hosts, records alerts when a host stops sending or a declared proof regresses, and issues share links you create. It does nothing on your machines and holds no credentials to them.
